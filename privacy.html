Privacy Policy

Pipe Riders · Callisto Solutions · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pipe Riders is a single-player 3D pipe racing game. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

Who we are

Pipe Riders is published by Callisto Solutions. Contact: rahmanik1228@gmail.com

What the app stores

Pipe Riders may keep a few settings on your device only (local storage), such as best score, progress, or mute preferences. This information never leaves your device. We do not operate a server that receives it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email, or sign-in
  • No ads, ad IDs, or tracking SDKs
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or location
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • No in-app purchases in this build

Internet

The game is designed to play offline. It does not require an internet connection for core gameplay.

Children

Pipe Riders does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
